SPECIAL TEAMS INTELLIGENCE V8.8

PLAYER INTELLIGENCE FIXES

- Player Intelligence is now one of the first visible mobile navigation buttons.
- Mobile navigation shows ALL sections in a compact three-column grid instead of hiding later items off-screen.
- The app automatically detects the primary team from the two Supabase PFF files.
- Player Intelligence works even if roster.json has not been created yet.
- Kickers, punters and returners are identified directly from PFF.
- Before roster sync, cards show team + jersey number and full PFF statistics.
- After roster.json exists, the same cards automatically gain official name, position, photo, hometown and bio information.
- Staff users no longer see roster-management controls inside Player Intelligence.

No change is required to the two PFF files already in Supabase.
